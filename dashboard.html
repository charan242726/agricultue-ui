📘 How to Use Fertilizers

  • Apply fertilizers during early growth stage 🌱
  • Mix fertilizers evenly in soil
  • Water field after applying fertilizer 💧
  • Do not overuse - it can damage crops ❌

⚖️ Recommended Quantities (per acre)

🌿 Urea (Nitrogen) 50-100 kg
🌱 DAP (Phosphorus) 40-60 kg
🌾 MOP (Potassium) 20-40 kg

⚠️ Tips

  • Check soil test before applying fertilizers
  • Use organic compost for better soil health
  • Split fertilizer application into 2-3 stages
